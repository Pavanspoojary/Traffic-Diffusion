Optimal: Capacity-Aware Multi-Corridor Split

Recommended Strategy: 'Capacity-Aware Multi-Corridor Split (Balanced Detour)' achieves optimal network relief, saving 242.8 vehicle-hours (-94.8% vs baseline) with peak arterial V/C at 1.00.

Trade-off Analysis: Creates zero new saturated bottlenecks. Requires 2 field units (Multi-Junction VMS Signage & Dynamic Guidance).
Control Room Advisory: Model estimates assist situational decision-making. Field deployment requires traffic controller authorization.
Model Assumptions & Standards
  • Capacities derived from IRC standards (900 PCU/lane/hr).
  • Travel times follow Bureau of Public Roads (BPR) volume-delay curves: t = t0 * [1 + 0.15*(V/C)^4], bounded at 8.0x crawl speed.
  • Delays and delay savings measured over a 1-hour operational analysis horizon.
  • Strictly isolated network initial conditions across all candidate scenarios.
